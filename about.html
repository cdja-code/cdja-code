--- layout: single title: About permalink: / author_profile: true classes: wide --- Hello! I am Chris Auckland, a physical oceanographer and postdoctoral research associate in the Centre for Ocean and Atmospheric Sciences at the University of East Anglia. I am currently working on the eSWEETS$^3$ project (enabling Sustainable Wind Energy Expansion in Seasonally Stratified Seas), investigating the impacts of floating offshore structures in the seasonally stratified North Sea. My research focusses on the use of autonomous underwater gliders to investigate changes in mixing and stratification in shelf seas.



Prior to this, I completed a PhD at the University of Southampton, hosted at the British Antarctic Survey, researching the transport of Antarctic Bottom Water from the Weddell Sea to the South Atlantic. This work used a combination of long-term moorings at a key outflow site, Orkney Passage, to diagnose changes in AABW volume and temperature in response to changing gyre and through flow strengths controlled by wind forcing. This was complimented by a suite of idealised MITgcm simulations as part of a placement at Stanford University.
